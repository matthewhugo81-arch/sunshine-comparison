Sunshine comparison

Retired · 12 October 2026

Sunshine forecast production has stopped.

This experiment has not established a sufficiently reliable, like-for-like comparison of bright-sunshine hours. The forecast maps have been withdrawn rather than keep publishing unverified estimates.

Not an operational forecast. Automated data retrieval is disabled. Saved data and previous versions are retained for research and must not be treated as current guidance.

Why it was retired

The current feeds estimate sunshine duration from time-averaged solar radiation. Those averages do not identify how many minutes the direct sun actually exceeded the sunshine threshold. Adding cloud percentages or changing thresholds without observational verification would produce another unvalidated estimate, not a demonstrated improvement.

The 12 October review found no summation mismatch in 700 station-day totals. It also confirmed that the retained snapshot did not provide complete UKMO Global or UKV sunshine totals for 14 October, preventing the intended four-model comparison for that date.

What the EPD disagreement tells us

EPD is another forecast, not an observation. The retained EPD examples were supplied on 10 October; their original issue cycle and exact accumulation definitions are unverified. Disagreement warrants investigation, but matching those numbers would not by itself demonstrate forecast accuracy.

What has been preserved

The repository, calculation scripts, tests, cloud diagnostics, source investigations and saved data remain available. The previous dashboard is preserved in version history. Other weather websites are unaffected.

Condition for reconsideration

Revisit only with documented, accessible sunshine-duration products for the named models, or a derived method that demonstrates useful skill against independent observations at matched stations, seasons and forecast lead times. No restart is scheduled.
